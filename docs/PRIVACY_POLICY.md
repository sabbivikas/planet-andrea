# Planet Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Planet ("the app") is a social night-out planning app: friend groups
("planets") swipe on local activities, vote in "battles" to pick the plan,
and can merge with other crews heading to the same place. Businesses can post
deals. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you
have.

## What we collect

- **Account information**: email address, display name, username, and profile
  photo when you create an account.
- **Contacts**: with your permission, your device contacts, used only to help
  you find and invite friends who also use the app.
- **Location**: with your permission, your approximate or precise location,
  used to show nearby activities, venues on the map, and distance-based
  deals. Location is only read while you use the app.
- **Photos and camera**: with your permission, photos you choose to share and
  photos you take in the app (for example a profile or group photo).
- **Identity verification**: if you choose to verify your account, the
  information needed to complete verification (which may include a
  government-issued ID). Verification status is stored; ID images are used
  only for the verification check.
- **Content you create**: groups, votes, battle results, chat messages,
  invites, and deals you interact with.
- **Usage analytics**: app interactions and crash/diagnostic data (PostHog)
  used to fix bugs and improve the app.
- **Purchase information**: if you buy something in the app, the purchase is
  processed by the app store and RevenueCat/Stripe; we store your purchase
  and entitlement status, never your card number.

## How we use it

To run the app (accounts, groups, battles, chat, maps, deals), to show you
relevant nearby activities, to keep the app secure (verification, abuse
prevention), and to understand and improve usage.

## What we share

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service
providers that run the app: Supabase (database, auth, file storage),
PostHog (analytics), RevenueCat and Stripe (purchases), and Resend
(transactional email). Other users see your profile, groups, and content
according to the app's social features.

## Your choices

- **Permissions**: contacts, location, camera, and photo access can be
  revoked any time in your device settings; the app keeps working with
  reduced functionality.
- **Account deletion**: you can delete your account at any time in the app
  (Settings > Delete Account). This deletes your profile, groups, messages,
  and other account data from our systems.

## Data retention and security

Account data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete
your account. Data is stored with Supabase (Postgres, encrypted at rest) and
transmitted over TLS.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data.
